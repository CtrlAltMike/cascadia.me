## Preservation rules

- Do not delete, rename, overwrite, regenerate, replace, redraw, recolor, or destructively crop a baseline file without explicit owner approval.
- Preserve masters, optimized derivatives, mobile compositions, and social compositions together.
- Every composition currently used by HTML, CSS, or social metadata retains an appropriate production role as the editorial architecture changes.
- Preserve `home-lenses.png` and `home-lenses.webp` as a locked alternate. They are currently dormant and are distinct from the active `home-lenses-fire` composition.
- Keep all six concept boards as approved design records. They remain references rather than production page artwork.
- New artwork or exports are additive. Use new filenames and do not silently substitute them for a locked asset.
- Responsive layout may reposition or non-destructively mask an image only when its subject, action, and explanatory meaning remain intact. The canonical asset remains unchanged.
- Captions, alt text, placement, and surrounding prose may change to support the new editorial model.
- Before completing each implementation phase, verify that all baseline files remain present and that every previously used composition remains referenced.

These rules supersede earlier language treating the subject or framing of existing artwork as flexible. That flexibility applies only to future artwork explicitly approved by the owner.
