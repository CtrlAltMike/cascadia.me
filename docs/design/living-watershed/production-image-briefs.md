## Household capability hero

**Asset family:** `assets/living-watershed/kit/household-capability-hero*`

Create a bold 2:1 Living Watershed panorama that turns preparedness into continuity: ocean and braided river through evergreen foothills to snowy mountains, with an ordinary home and small neighborhood nested into the watershed. Subtle pathways connect water, safe warmth, electric light, radio communication, evacuation, food, medicine, animals, and neighbors without becoming a product catalogue. Protect the left 42 percent as warm-cream copy space and preserve the right-center home for the mobile crop. Use gouache, transparent watercolor, paper grain, relief-map contours, and the approved palette. No text, active disaster, shopping layout, luxury staging, cultural motifs, or glossy 3D rendering.

## Household rehearsal

**Asset family:** `assets/living-watershed/kit/household-rehearsal*`

Show two adults, a school-age child, and an older neighbor who uses a cane calmly rehearsing household continuity around a kitchen table. A paper route map, reachable water, lantern, radio, power bank, medicine pouch, familiar food, sanitation supplies, sturdy shoes, cat carrier, and compact departure bag support the decisions without becoming the subject. The setting is an ordinary Pacific Northwest home with foothills outside. Use a 3:2 editorial composition, natural interaction, and quiet contour motifs. No readable text, branded products, tactical gear, luxury styling, crisis spectacle, or cultural motifs.

## Guides orientation hero

**Asset family:** `assets/living-watershed/surfaces/guides-hero*`

Create one continuous 2:1 Cascadian landscape that makes Land, Water, Weather, and Fire legible as one system. Move from snowy mountains through forests and braided rivers to coast; include a subtle fault section, floodplain, storm bands, and a small distant ridge fire connected by contour and flow lines. Keep the image luminous, calm, alert, and saturated without depicting homes in danger. No text, icons, map labels, disaster spectacle, invented cultural ornament, or watermark.

## Approach trust illustration

**Asset family:** `assets/living-watershed/surfaces/approach-trust*`

Create a 3:2 tactile editorial illustration showing how an observation becomes careful public guidance. Rain, river, weather, and seismic instruments on the left feed translucent maps and contour layers in the center; the layers resolve on the right into bounded household decisions involving a home, road, and neighbors. Visible margins and incomplete edges communicate limits rather than false certainty. Use gouache, watercolor, paper grain, scientific instruments, and the Living Watershed palette. No readable text, fake agency seals, software dashboard, catastrophe, invented cultural ornament, or watermark.
