How to read the ferries map

Positions lag the water

Vessels report their position every few seconds, but the feed, its cache, and this page's roughly 30-second refresh each add delay. A marker is a recent report, not the boat itself. Faded markers are running on positions more than five minutes old.

ETAs and departures are estimates

Arrival and departure times come from Washington State Ferries' own estimates and shift with loading, weather, traffic, and crewing. Sailings can be delayed or canceled after this map last checked. Confirm against the official schedule and terminal alerts before you drive to a dock.

A missing vessel is not a missing sailing

Only in-service vessels that are reporting positions appear. Boats in the yard, tied up for the night, or with a quiet transponder drop off the map without meaning service has stopped — and a vessel shown underway says nothing about space for your car.

Terminals are reference points

Terminal dots mark approximate dock locations for orientation, not property lines, holding lanes, or tide conditions. The Anacortes–Sidney B.C. international route remains suspended, so its terminal appears without vessels.

Not for navigation

This map is for shoreside awareness. It is not a navigation product, and it does not replace VesselWatch, VHF traffic, or the judgment of anyone actually on the water.
