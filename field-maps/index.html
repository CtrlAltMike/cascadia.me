A different map product

How Field Maps differs from Atlas and Signals

Field Maps helps you obtain an official map for terrain, recreation, access, or offline use. The Regional Hazard Atlas compares selected reports, observations, forecasts, and planning layers. Signals helps identify the public organizations responsible for a place.

Those products can point toward one another, but none is evidence that another product’s source is complete, current, or suitable for a different job.

Field Maps remains list-first.

  • No basemap is required to find a source.
  • No location permission is requested.
  • No route, trail, or household choice is stored.
  • No private-company map validates the catalog.

Publisher before interface

The authority changes with the question.

These source families establish the first U.S. proof. Every regional record carries a publisher, format, review date, offline method, limitation, and direct official route.

U.S. Geological Survey

US Topo

Free official terrain source

Use current quadrangle maps for contours, water, names, roads, selected trails, and a printable terrain reference. Features inside one sheet can have different source dates.

National Park Service

Park and wilderness maps

Free official recreation source

Use park maps for areas, destinations, facilities, wilderness camps, and park planning. Pair them with current trail, road, tide, permit, fire, and weather information.

Open Olympic National Park maps Read the map record

USDA Forest Service

Motor Vehicle Use Maps

Free official access source

Use the MVUM for legal motor-vehicle designations on National Forest System roads and trails. It does not establish passability, maintenance, temporary closures, or topographic context.

A map is not a condition report

Keep changing facts at the source.

Closures, fire restrictions, weather, tides, ferries, permits, and bridge or road status change on a different clock from the map file.

Good durable map content

  • Terrain and contours
  • Publisher and edition
  • Map symbols and scale
  • Land-manager context

Check live before leaving

  • Trail and road closures
  • Fire and weather restrictions
  • Tides, ferries, and permits
  • Facility and bridge status

Practice without service

Download the file, open it in airplane mode, print a non-battery backup, and make sure everyone who needs the map can reach it.

Coverage honesty

One proof, not a claim of everywhere.

The adopted Cascadia boundary includes Washington, Oregon, and British Columbia. The official U.S. grid has been enumerated, but the public finder begins with one regional proof while exact files and source families are reviewed.

British Columbia’s official open-government sources will remain free-finder candidates. They are not treated as public-domain paid inventory.
