Land manager

Identify the responsible land manager

The Olympic Peninsula includes national park, national forest, state, tribal, local, and private lands. Start with the publisher shown on the map, then confirm the responsible organization for the exact place and activity.

For national park questions, use Olympic National Park. For national forest access, use Olympic National Forest. For a broader directory of public organizations, continue to Cascadia Signals.

A boundary on a map is a lead.

  • It may not be a surveyed property boundary.
  • Rules can change across adjacent jurisdictions.
  • Tribal lands are governed by their respective nations.
  • Ask the responsible office when the answer matters.
