    Please read this before continuing.

    Signals is a very early directory assembled from official public registries, agency websites, and outside geometry services. Alert programs and responsibilities change; names, links, and service areas can still be incomplete, unavailable, out of date, or wrong.

    Signals does not report current emergencies. Use it to reach responsible organizations, then follow official alerts and instructions for urgent decisions.

    What the optional outlines mean

    Signals maps a service or jurisdiction outline only when an official, relevant geometry is available. Overlay checkboxes affect the map alone; the directory remains complete for the current view.

    No invented coverage

    When a resource has no defensible service geometry, Signals keeps the listing and omits its perimeter. Legal boundaries are only a geographic index; the record’s coverage note controls the interpretation. Signals contains no live-incident or hand-drawn emergency polygons.
